Pinned site sync

Off by default. Chrome must be running. Alarm timing can be delayed. Only granted sites sync; localStorage needs an already-open tab at the pinned origin. No tabs are opened automatically.

    Privacy and limits

    Only domains and counts appear in transfer results. Cookie and storage values are sent to the relay, never displayed. Manual credential entry is not supported.

    Saved Steel profiles contain plaintext session data on the relay host. Domain removal changes saved cookies and localStorage only. It does not sign out active Steel sessions or remove the separate credential store.

    Revoke site or relay access in Chrome extension settings. The context menu and Alt+Shift+S shortcut use your last selected profile and granted site access. Change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
